Your score in Erase is the number of cards you have left. Could everyone always score zero? Under the printed rules you erase pairs that add to 10. Even with perfect play, {{pairs.zero.percent:.2f}}% of deals leave cards behind. Let a player add as many cards as they like and the share drops to {{addition.zero.percent:.2f}}%.

Now allow subtraction and multiplication too. A zero can take any spare cards with it: multiply them by zero and add the result to your 10. Counting every deal under these rules leaves only {{deals.stuck}} stuck deals out of {{deals.total}}, about one in {{deals.one_in}}. Every other deal can be cleared in one group.

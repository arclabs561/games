Which deals of this make-ten card game cannot be cleared? The answer depends on how the rules are read. The printed rules erase pairs that sum to 10. Under them {{pairs.zero.percent:.2f}}% of deals are stuck, and with addition alone {{addition.zero.percent:.2f}}% are. Under the most generous reading of the sheet's variation, which combines any number of cards with +, − and ×, only {{deals.stuck}} of the {{deals.total}} possible deals are stuck, about 1 in {{deals.one_in}}. All three counts are exact, and the last comes with a proof and machine-checked certificates. The rest of this page is about that reading. There, a deal that can be cleared can always be cleared in one group, and a stuck hand without a 0 is caught by a remainder argument, mod 3 or mod 4. Ten is not special: for every target from 1 to 30, the hands that cannot make it are caught in the same way from {{targets.thresholds:or}} cards on, checked through {{targets.max_cards}} cards.

# C3's givers and the count-1 rule, scored by language-model guessers (plan C13)

Detail behind the [Codenames README](../README.md). Every number is in [c13.json](c13.json), written by `python -m codenames.c13 score` in a `cpu-basic` job from the guess lines in the private dataset (`runs/c13/`), and from [c13-lists.json](c13-lists.json) and [c13-picks.txt](c13-picks.txt), which `c13 lists` wrote here before any model call. Jobs and costs are in [c13-runs.json](c13-runs.json).

## Findings

- **H-self holds in the other direction.** Population training minus self-play, averaged over the two givers, the two language-model guessers and the five training seeds, is −0.148 words per turn (−0.258 to −0.035) on 375 boards. Holm's correction rejects 0 (p = 0.011). With gpt-oss-120b and Llama 3.3 70B as strangers, the self-play giver does better.
- **H-rule holds.** Population training minus the count-1 rule is +0.114 (0.065 to 0.168), p < 0.0002, Holm rejects 0. The interval is not inside the plan's equivalence margin of ±0.10, so the two are not shown equivalent; it excludes 0, and population training is the better of the two here.
- **The embedding reference repeats C3.** Scored exactly by each task's held-out embedding guesser on the same 400 boards, population minus self-play is +0.219 (0.154 to 0.285) for potion (bge held out) and +0.350 (0.286 to 0.416) for Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B (EmbeddingGemma 2 held out), and population minus count-1 is +0.025 (0.006 to 0.044) and −0.017 (−0.048 to 0.014). The EAT guesser agrees: +0.314 and +0.363 for population minus self-play.
- **What changes is the count.** Self-play asks for 2.99 (potion) and 2.84 (Qwen3) words on average, population training 1.13 and 1.21, count-1 one, and the untrained start 4. Under the embedding guessers the safer count is worth more than the lost words; under the language models it is worth less. C11 found the same with C10's population pick.

These are model partners, a proxy for people, as METHOD.md says. One task per giver was scored.

## Design

As in `experiments/plans/C13.md`. Test boards: 400 of seed 13004; pilot: 10 of seed 13999, not scored. Each giver plays its C3 headline task. Every arm's pick comes from C3's committed theta, greedy, with the feature scale of that seed's training boards; `c13 lists` checked the picks against C3's committed picks on C3's first three test boards. Each guesser hears C4's guesser prompt with the action's count, once per distinct (board, clue, count): 2,431 prompts, 6.08 distinct actions per board. Primary intervals: the stratified bootstrap of METHOD.md, boards resampled and, within each giver, its five seeds resampled with population and self-play of one seed as a pair (10,000 resamples, seed 0). The detectable effect at Holm's level was 0.17 for H-self and 0.07 for H-rule, from the test's own standard deviations (1.08 and 0.44); the plan expected 0.17.

## Replies, repeat check and cost

gpt-oss-120b (DeepInfra, reasoning effort low, 1,024 tokens) returned an empty first reply on 208 of 2,431 prompts and 4 failed; all were sent once more, and 25 stayed empty. 73 replies (3.0%) reached the cap and still named board words. Llama 3.3 (Novita, 256 tokens) answered every prompt after retries on "server overload" errors. Every reply came from the pinned provider. The repeat check sent the prompts of the first three boards twice more: the played-out value changed on 7 of 72 prompts for gpt-oss-120b and 6 of 72 for Llama 3.3. The pilot sent 14 of its 58 prompts per model before the meter's reservation stopped it; it was used only to check reading, providers and cost.

The jobs spent $0.178 by the meter: pilot $0.001, gpt-oss-120b $0.107, Llama 3.3 $0.065, repeat check $0.003, plus `cpu-basic` time for scoring. The plan expected about $0.40, under a cap of $0.70.

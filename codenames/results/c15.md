# Has gpt-oss-120b memorized Cultural Codes? (plan C15)

Detail behind the [Codenames README](../README.md). Every number here is in [c15.json](c15.json), written by `python -m codenames.c15 score` in a `cpu-basic` job from the guess lines in the private dataset (`runs/c15/`); jobs, commits and costs are in [c15-runs.json](c15-runs.json). The turns' boards are not committed: Cultural Codes states no license.

## Findings

- **No sign of memorization.** On 2,009 turns from C1's training games (263 games), gpt-oss-120b's agreement with the person is 0.678 on the board as published (O) and 0.730 when every word but the person's pick is swapped for a noun of the same frequency (W). EmbeddingGemma 2 agrees 0.597 on both. The primary difference in differences, [model's O − W] − [EmbeddingGemma 2's O − W], is −0.052 (−0.077 to −0.028), 10,000 resamples of games, seed 0. The plan reads an interval above 0 as evidence that the model's lead depends on the published board; this one lies below 0. It is not inside the equivalence margin of ±0.03, so the probe does not show the effect to be zero: the change helps the model.
- **Order alone changes nothing.** Shuffling the words (S) moves the model's agreement by −0.004 (−0.015 to 0.007) relative to the control, inside the margin of ±0.03. Llama 3.3 70B behaves the same way on its 1,004 turns: −0.005 (−0.027 to 0.017) for S, and −0.063 (−0.094 to −0.031) for W, with agreement 0.640, 0.645 and 0.703.
- **Direct traces are rare.** On W, a reply named a word of the original board that the swapped board did not show on 12 of gpt-oss-120b's 2,009 turns and 7 of Llama 3.3's 1,004. These were counted, not read, and a common word can turn up by chance.
- **Why W is easier for the language models** is not settled here. The swap keeps the clue, the pick, the pick's position and each word's frequency, and the embedding guessers do not notice it.

## Design

As in `experiments/plans/C15.md` and its amendment of 2026-11-20 (the swap pool, the turns, the pilot), written before any model call.

- **Turns.** C1's training games, in the order of seed 15001, taken whole until there are at least 2,000 turns: 2,009 turns in 263 games; Llama 3.3 the first 1,004 turns. These are not C1's test turns, so this is not a use of that test set.
- **Arms.** O, the board as published; S, shuffled (seed 15002 and the turn's index); W, shuffled and every word but the pick replaced by a uniform draw among the WordNet nouns of 3 to 9 letters with vectors here whose Zipf frequency is within 0.1 (seed 15003). All 42,810 replacements were within the window.
- **Guessers.** gpt-oss-120b pinned to DeepInfra (reasoning effort low, 1,024 tokens) and Llama 3.3 70B pinned to Novita (256 tokens), temperature 0, seed 0, C4's guesser prompt with count 1. An empty, failed or unpinned reply was sent once more at the end of its job; a reply that names no board word is not a guess. H-memory uses the turns the model answered in both arms (1 turn dropped). The controls are EmbeddingGemma 2 with C1's calibrated templates, computed exactly, and potion with C1's linear map (five seeds), which was trained on these games and is secondary.

## Replies, cost and repeat check

| Arm | gpt-oss-120b: agreement (95% Wilson) | empty first replies, resent | cut at 1,024 | Llama 3.3: agreement |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| O | 0.678 (0.657 to 0.698) | 134 | 29 | 0.640 (0.610 to 0.670) |
| S | 0.681 (0.661 to 0.701) | 160 | 32 | 0.645 (0.615 to 0.674) |
| W | 0.730 (0.710 to 0.749) | 16 | 45 | 0.703 (0.674 to 0.730) |

gpt-oss-120b returned an empty first reply on 134 and 160 of the O and S prompts and on 16 of the W prompts; all were answered when sent again, except one O turn that named no board word. A reply cut at the cap still counts when it names board words (29 to 45 per arm, 1.4% to 2.2%). The repeat check sent the first 20 turns of each arm twice more: the first pick changed on 0, 1 and 3 of 20 prompts for gpt-oss-120b (O, S, W) and on 8, 1 and 0 for Llama 3.3. Novita answered Llama 3.3 with "server overload" errors on many calls, and those were retried. Every reply came from the pinned provider.

The jobs spent $0.334 by the meter (reported cost where the provider gave one, otherwise reported tokens times the listed price): pilot $0.004, gpt-oss-120b $0.225, Llama 3.3 $0.096, repeat check $0.008, plus `cpu-basic` time for the scoring job. The plan expected about $0.40, under a cap of $0.70.

## Threats

As the plan lists them: the swap keeps the clue and the pick, so memory of those is not tested; the swap changes how hard a turn is in ways an embedding may not share, which is what W shows here; only top-1 agreement is scored; and every guesser is a model, so this says nothing about people.

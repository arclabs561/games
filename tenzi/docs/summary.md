How many turns does a game of Tenzi take, and what is the best way to play? Keeping the face that is most common after the first roll, a game takes {{strategies.fixed face.mean:.2f}} turns on average; switching faces when another group grows larger lowers that to {{strategies.switching.mean:.2f}}. No strategy does better on average, and none is more likely to be done by any turn up to {{deadline.last}}, after which no strategy can gain more than 10<sup>−12</sup>. So in a race at equal pace against players who do not react to our dice, switching wins most often, to within 10<sup>−12</sup>. Exact, by a Markov chain and a Markov decision process, and checked by simulation. That keeping the most common face is best on average was found before for one to seven dice (Puzzling Stack Exchange, 2020); the proof for ten dice, the deadlines and the race are added here.

How many turns does a game of Tenzi take, and what is the best way to play? Keeping the face that is most common after the first roll, a game takes {{strategies.fixed face.mean:.2f}} turns on average; switching faces when another group grows larger lowers that to {{strategies.switching.mean:.2f}}. No strategy does better, either on average or in the chance of being done by any given turn, so switching also wins races most often. Exact, by a Markov chain and a Markov decision process, and checked by simulation.
